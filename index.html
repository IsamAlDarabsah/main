About

I am interested in mathematical biology and nonlinear dynamical systems. As an applied mathematician, I aim to use mathematical tools to understand different phenomena by explaining observed dynamics and predicting novel dynamics.

I particularly seek problems that arise in immunology, epidemiology, ecology, and neural networks. In my research, I study models that involve nonlinear ordinary or delay differential equations. I use dynamical systems and perturbation theory methods to study the models analytically and computationally.
